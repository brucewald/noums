noums has a new home: noums.net

noums moved to its own address. Old links still work and send you here automatically. Email sign-in now works for everyone, and sign-in emails come from noums.

September 2026

Presentation mode

Rehearse a class presentation, speech or pitch against your own talking points. Step through them as you go, and the recap shows which points you covered, how long each took, and how you did against your target time.

August 2026

More accurate “um” counting

Browsers clean up microphone audio for phone calls, and that cleanup was quietly muffling the “ums” noums is trying to count. noums now listens to your microphone without that filtering, so far fewer fillers slip through.

July 2026

noums launches

The first version went live: interview and free-talk practice, a live filler counter, and a recap with your fluency score. Within the month it gained accounts with Google and email sign-in, history that syncs between devices, mic calibration, and a second, more accurate filler check after each session.
